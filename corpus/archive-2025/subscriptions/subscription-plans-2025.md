# Archived 2025 Copy: Subscription Plans and Pricing

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered subscription plans and pricing before the 2026 policy consolidation. This reference describes the BrightCart Plus subscription options used by support and checkout. It does not list temporary promotional pricing.

## Archived rule

BrightCart Plus is offered as a monthly plan and an annual plan. The annual plan is priced 20 percent lower than paying the standard monthly price for twelve months. Both plans provide the same core member benefits unless a promotion explicitly adds a temporary benefit.

## Eligibility details

- Taxes may apply based on customer location.
- Promotional discounts are applied separately from the standard annual savings.
- Plan changes take effect according to the billing change shown at checkout.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

If the standard monthly price is $25, twelve months would total $240 before tax. The standard annual plan would be priced at $204 before tax because it includes the 15 percent annual savings.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Corporate memberships use negotiated pricing.
- Gift memberships are outside this level one corpus.
- A temporary campaign can change the displayed first year price without changing the normal annual discount rule.
