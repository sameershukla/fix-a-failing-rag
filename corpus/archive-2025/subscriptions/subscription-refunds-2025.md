# Archived 2025 Copy: Subscription Refund Eligibility

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered subscription refund eligibility before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy defines the narrow cases in which a recently renewed BrightCart Plus subscription can be refunded. It should be used separately from the cancellation process.

## Archived rule

An accidental subscription renewal can be refunded when the customer requests the refund within 72 hours of the renewal charge and has not used a paid membership benefit after that renewal. When approved, the subscription is canceled and the latest renewal charge is refunded.

## Eligibility details

- The request must be within 72 hours of the renewal timestamp.
- No paid membership benefit can have been used after renewal.
- The refund applies to the most recent renewal, not earlier billing periods.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

An annual plan renews at 14:00 AM Monday. The customer contacts support Tuesday morning and has not used any member benefit since renewal. The renewal meets the standard refund conditions.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Charges older than 72 hours require supervisor review and are normally not refundable.
- Fraud claims use the payment dispute process.
- Promotional credits are not refunded as cash.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the subscriptions operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.
