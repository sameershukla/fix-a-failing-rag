# Archived 2025 Copy: Pausing and Resuming Membership

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered pausing and resuming membership before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy allows some customers to temporarily pause BrightCart Plus instead of canceling. It applies only to standard monthly memberships.

## Archived rule

A monthly BrightCart Plus subscription can be paused for 1 or 2 full billing months. Annual plans cannot be paused. During a pause, recurring charges stop and membership benefits are inactive. The subscription resumes automatically after the selected pause period.

## Eligibility details

- The plan must be monthly and in good standing.
- A pause starts at the end of the current paid billing period.
- Only one active pause can exist at a time.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A monthly member whose next billing date is May 20 chooses a two month pause on May 2. Benefits remain active until May 15, stay paused for two billing months, and resume on July 15.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Past due subscriptions cannot be paused until payment status is resolved.
- Annual plans can be canceled but not paused.
- Promotional trials may have their own pause restrictions.
