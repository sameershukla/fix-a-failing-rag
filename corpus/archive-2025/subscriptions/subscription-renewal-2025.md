# Archived 2025 Copy: Subscription Renewal Notices

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered subscription renewal notices before the 2026 policy consolidation. This article describes automatic renewal and the reminder BrightCart sends for annual plans. Monthly subscriptions renew without a separate monthly reminder.

## Archived rule

BrightCart Plus subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. For annual plans, BrightCart sends a renewal reminder 12 days before the renewal date to the account email address. The reminder includes the scheduled renewal date and the current renewal price.

## Eligibility details

- The email address on the account must be valid to receive the reminder.
- The renewal date is visible in account settings.
- Updating the payment method does not change the renewal date.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

An annual subscription renews on October 25. BrightCart sends the reminder on October 13. If the customer cancels on October 18, the renewal is stopped and benefits end on October 20.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Corporate plans can use contract specific notices.
- A bounced email does not automatically stop renewal.
- Emergency service notices are separate from renewal reminders.
