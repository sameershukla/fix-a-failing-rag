# Archived 2025 Copy: Canceling a Subscription

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered canceling a subscription before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy explains what happens when a customer cancels BrightCart Plus. Cancellation stops future renewal but does not automatically reverse a charge that already occurred.

## Archived rule

A cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. The customer keeps subscription benefits until that date and the plan does not renew. Cancellation by itself does not create a refund for the current period.

## Eligibility details

- The account must have an active subscription.
- The cancellation confirmation shows the benefit end date.
- Future renewal is disabled immediately after cancellation.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A monthly plan renews on the 10th. The customer cancels on the 18th. Benefits continue until the next 10th, and there is no new charge on that date. The charge from the current period is not automatically refunded.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- A qualifying accidental renewal can be refunded under the subscription refund policy.
- A fraud related subscription charge follows the payment dispute process.
- Account deletion does not replace the need to cancel a subscription before deletion.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the subscriptions operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.
