# Archived 2025 Copy: Damaged Item Claims

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered damaged item claims before the 2026 policy consolidation. This runbook covers merchandise that arrives physically damaged. It helps support distinguish delivery damage from customer caused damage and collect enough information for a replacement or refund decision.

## Archived rule

Customers should report visible delivery damage within 10 calendar days of delivery. Support should request clear photos of the item and shipping package when practical. The customer should not ship the damaged item back until BrightCart provides return instructions or confirms that no return is required.

## Eligibility details

- The order number and affected item must be identified.
- Photos are requested when they can reasonably show the damage.
- Safety issues such as leaking batteries should be escalated immediately.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A glass coffee carafe arrives cracked on Friday and the customer reports it on Sunday with photos of the broken carafe and crushed carton. Support can process the damaged item workflow without waiting for a normal return inspection.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- If a customer cannot safely handle the product, do not require extra photos.
- Hidden damage discovered after 12 days can still be reviewed but may require supervisor approval.
- Normal wear after use is not delivery damage.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the returns operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about damaged item claims.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.

## Common customer wording

Customers rarely use policy names. They may say that something is taking too long, that a charge or shipment looks wrong, or that a support agent gave them a different answer. Those phrases can make this archived page look relevant to a modern query.
