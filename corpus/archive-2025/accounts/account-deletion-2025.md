# Archived 2025 Copy: Customer Account Deletion

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered customer account deletion before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy explains what happens after a customer requests deletion of a BrightCart account. It separates user visible account removal from data that must be retained for legal or financial reasons.

## Archived rule

After identity verification, BrightCart schedules account deletion and completes the customer facing deletion within 35 days. Certain transaction, tax, fraud, and legal records can be retained when required by law or legitimate business obligations even after the account is no longer active.

## Eligibility details

- Identity verification is required before deletion starts.
- Active subscription renewal should be canceled before the account is closed.
- Open refunds or disputes may need to finish before some data is archived.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A verified customer requests deletion on August 6. BrightCart disables normal account use and completes the deletion process by August 31, while retaining required invoice records in restricted systems for the legally required period.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- A legal hold can delay deletion of specific records.
- Fraud investigation evidence may be retained under security policy.
- Deleting an account does not erase data held by an independent bank or carrier.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the accounts operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about customer account deletion.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.

Customers rarely use policy names. They may say that something is taking too long, that a charge or shipment looks wrong, or that a support agent gave them a different answer. Those phrases can make this archived page look relevant to a modern query.
