# Archived 2025 Copy: Privacy Deletion Requests

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered privacy deletion requests before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy covers privacy requests asking BrightCart to delete eligible personal data. It complements, but is broader than, normal account closure.

## Archived rule

BrightCart verifies the requester before processing a privacy deletion request and targets completion within 35 days. Data required for tax, fraud prevention, legal claims, or other mandatory retention can be kept in restricted systems for the required period.

## Eligibility details

- The request must be linked to a verified identity.
- The privacy team tracks completion across source systems.
- Open disputes can create a retention requirement.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer requests privacy deletion after closing the account. BrightCart removes eligible profile and preference data, while retaining required invoice and fraud records in restricted systems until their retention periods expire.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- A legal hold overrides routine deletion for covered records.
- Backups can age out according to the backup retention schedule rather than immediate live deletion.
- Data controlled solely by another company follows that company's process.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the privacy operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about privacy deletion requests.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.

## Common customer wording

Customers rarely use policy names. They may say that something is taking too long, that a charge or shipment looks wrong, or that a support agent gave them a different answer. Those phrases can make this archived page look relevant to a modern query.
