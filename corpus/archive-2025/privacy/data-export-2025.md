# Archived 2025 Copy: Customer Data Export

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered customer data export before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy describes the self service export a verified customer can request for a copy of common BrightCart account data.

## Archived rule

A verified customer data export is normally prepared within 12 hours. When the export is ready, BrightCart sends a download link that remains valid for 72 hours. The link requires the customer to sign in again before download.

## Eligibility details

- The request must come from the authenticated account.
- Only data tied to the requesting account is included.
- Large accounts can take longer and will show a pending status.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer requests an export at 3:00 PM Tuesday. The file is prepared Wednesday morning and the customer receives a link. If the link is not used within 72 hours, a new export request is required.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Data under legal restriction can be excluded or delayed.
- Payment processor data controlled by another company is not exported as raw card data.
- Business organization exports can require administrator approval.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the privacy operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about customer data export.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.

Customers rarely use policy names. They may say that something is taking too long, that a charge or shipment looks wrong, or that a support agent gave them a different answer. Those phrases can make this archived page look relevant to a modern query.
