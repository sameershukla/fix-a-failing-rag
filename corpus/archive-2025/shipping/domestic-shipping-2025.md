# Archived 2025 Copy: Domestic Shipping Service Levels

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered domestic shipping service levels before the 2026 policy consolidation. This reference lists the normal delivery expectation for shipments within the contiguous United States. It is used for checkout messaging and customer support estimates.

## Archived rule

Standard domestic shipping normally arrives in 5 to 8 business days after the order ships. The estimate begins when the carrier accepts the package, not when the customer places the order. Processing time before shipment is separate.

## Eligibility details

- Business days exclude weekends and federal holidays.
- The delivery estimate can vary for Alaska, Hawaii, and remote postal codes.
- A tracking number is created before the carrier may show the first scan.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

An order ships on Monday using standard service. With no holiday, the expected delivery range is Thursday through the following Monday. A Tuesday label creation without a carrier acceptance scan does not start the delivery clock.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Weather and carrier disruptions can extend the estimate.
- Backordered and preorder items have separate ship dates.
- Expedited service follows the expedited shipping article.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the shipping operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.
