# Archived 2025 Copy: Expedited Shipping

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered expedited shipping before the 2026 policy consolidation. This article describes the BrightCart two business day shipping option. It explains the order cutoff and what the service commitment does and does not include.

## Archived rule

Eligible expedited orders placed before 12:00 PM Central Time on a business day are expected to ship the same day and arrive within 3 business days after carrier acceptance. Orders placed after the cutoff are processed the next business day.

## Eligibility details

- The item must be in stock at a warehouse that supports expedited service.
- Saturday and Sunday are not counted as business days.
- Address verification can delay release of an order.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer places an eligible expedited order at 6:30 PM CT on Tuesday. The warehouse ships it Tuesday and the carrier should deliver by Thursday. The same order placed at 3:30 PM is expected to ship Wednesday and arrive by Friday.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Oversize items and hazardous materials are not eligible.
- Weather service alerts can suspend the commitment.
- A payment or fraud hold pauses fulfillment until cleared.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the shipping operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.
