# Archived 2025 Copy: International Shipping and Duties

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered international shipping and duties before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy explains normal international delivery timing and customer responsibility for import charges. It applies only to countries shown as eligible at checkout.

## Archived rule

International orders normally arrive in 10 to 18 business days after shipment. Import duties, taxes, and customs fees are the customer responsibility unless checkout explicitly states that duties are prepaid. Customs processing can add time outside BrightCart control.

## Eligibility details

- The destination country must be supported at checkout.
- Carrier tracking may pause while a package is in customs.
- The customer is responsible for accurate recipient and tax information.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A package ships from the United States to France and reaches customs after six business days. The carrier requests a local import tax before final delivery. The customer pays that fee because the order was not marked duties prepaid.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Some promotions include duties prepaid and state that explicitly on the order.
- Restricted products cannot be shipped to certain countries.
- Military and diplomatic addresses use a separate carrier route and may take longer.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the shipping operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about international shipping and duties.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.
