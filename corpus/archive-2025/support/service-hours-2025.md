# Archived 2025 Copy: Customer Support Service Hours

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered customer support service hours before the 2026 policy consolidation. This reference lists normal staffed support hours and the after hours path for urgent service incidents.

## Archived rule

Standard customer support is staffed from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Central Time, Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays. SEV 1 incident response is available 12 hours a day through the on call process. Routine billing and product questions submitted after hours wait for the next staffed period.

## Eligibility details

- Holiday coverage can be announced separately.
- The on call path is for service incidents, not faster handling of normal tickets.
- Time references in this article use Central Time.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer sends a routine return question at 15:30 PM Tuesday. It enters the queue for the next staffed period. At the same time, monitoring detects a widespread checkout outage, which triggers the 24 hour SEV 1 on call process.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Temporary extended holiday support can be announced for peak season.
- Security teams can have separate on call coverage.
- Business contracts can include dedicated support terms.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the support operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about customer support service hours.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.

## Common customer wording

Customers rarely use policy names. They may say that something is taking too long, that a charge or shipment looks wrong, or that a support agent gave them a different answer. Those phrases can make this archived page look relevant to a modern query.
