# Archived 2025 Copy: Partial Refunds and Deductions

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered partial refunds and deductions before the 2026 policy consolidation. Partial refunds are used when the customer is owed money but not the full original item price. This guide explains the allowed reasons for deductions and the information that must be shown to the customer.

## Archived rule

A partial refund may be issued when returned merchandise is missing customer removable parts, shows customer caused damage, or only part of a multi item order is being refunded. The deduction must be tied to a documented reason and the customer must be told the amount before the refund is finalized.

## Eligibility details

- Shipping fees are refunded only when the shipping service itself failed or the return was caused by BrightCart.
- Tax is recalculated based on the refunded merchandise amount.
- Agents cannot invent a restocking fee when no published fee applies.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer returns a $145 blender but does not return the $20 travel cup that was included in the package. After the warehouse confirms the missing accessory, the refund can be reduced by the documented replacement value of the cup.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Do not deduct for packaging that was opened as part of a normal inspection.
- Do not deduct for damage that was already reported as carrier damage.
- Any proposed deduction above $50 requires a supervisor review.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the refunds operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about partial refunds and deductions.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.
