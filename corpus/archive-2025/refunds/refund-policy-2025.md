# Archived 2025 Copy: Standard Refund Policy

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered standard refund policy before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy defines how BrightCart issues customer refunds after a return, cancellation, or approved service adjustment. It applies to consumer orders placed through the BrightCart web site and mobile app. Support agents should use this policy as the starting point before checking a product specific exception.

## Archived rule

Once BrightCart approves a refund, the refund is sent back to the original payment method. BrightCart normally issues the refund within 10 business days after the return is accepted or the cancellation is confirmed. Bank processing time can add additional days after BrightCart sends the money.

## Eligibility details

- The return or cancellation must already be approved.
- The original payment method should be used unless a separate policy explicitly says otherwise.
- Business days exclude weekends and United States federal holidays.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer returns a jacket on Monday and the warehouse accepts the return on Tuesday. BrightCart should issue the refund no later than the following Tuesday, assuming there is no payment processor failure. The customer may see the credit later if the card issuer takes extra time to post it.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Gift card purchases are refunded to the original gift card balance.
- Mixed payment orders follow the proportional refund rule in the gift card refund article.
- Chargebacks are handled by the payments team and are not normal refunds.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the refunds operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about standard refund policy.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.

## Common customer wording

Customers rarely use policy names. They may say that something is taking too long, that a charge or shipment looks wrong, or that a support agent gave them a different answer. Those phrases can make this archived page look relevant to a modern query.
