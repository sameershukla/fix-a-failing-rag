# Archived 2025 Copy: Refund Eligibility Rules

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered refund eligibility rules before the 2026 policy consolidation. This article explains when an item is eligible for a refund. It is used after a customer asks to return an order and before the return label or refund is approved. Product specific policies can be stricter than this general rule.

## Archived rule

Most unused physical items are eligible for a refund when the return is requested within 45 calendar days of delivery. Final sale items are not refundable. The item must include the parts and accessories that were shipped with it unless the item arrived damaged or incomplete.

## Eligibility details

- The 35 day clock starts on the carrier delivery date.
- Unused means the item has not been materially altered, consumed, or installed.
- A missing accessory can reduce the refund when the customer caused the loss.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer receives a kitchen scale on March 6 and requests a return on March 24. The scale is unused and includes the tray, batteries, and manual. The return is eligible under the standard rule.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Defective items can qualify under a product warranty even after the normal return window.
- Final sale merchandise remains nonrefundable unless it arrived damaged or the wrong item was shipped.
- Digital goods use a separate digital purchase policy and are outside this corpus.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the refunds operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about refund eligibility rules.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.

## Common customer wording

Customers rarely use policy names. They may say that something is taking too long, that a charge or shipment looks wrong, or that a support agent gave them a different answer. Those phrases can make this archived page look relevant to a modern query.
