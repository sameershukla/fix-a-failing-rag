# Archived 2025 Copy: Lost and Stolen Gift Cards

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered lost and stolen gift cards before the 2026 policy consolidation. This runbook covers claims that a gift card code was lost, deleted, or stolen. Gift cards are cash like instruments, so replacement requires evidence of ownership.

## Archived rule

BrightCart may replace a lost digital gift card only when the claimant can verify the original purchase or recipient information and the remaining balance has not been spent. Gift cards are not redeemable for cash and spent balances are not restored merely because the code was shared with another person.

## Eligibility details

- Verify order number or purchaser account when possible.
- Check redemption history before replacement.
- Freeze the remaining balance when fraud is reasonably suspected.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A buyer deleted the original email but can verify the gift card order and recipient address. The card still has its full balance. Support can resend or replace the code after verification.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- A fully redeemed card normally has no balance to replace.
- Confirmed account takeover cases are handled with security.
- Corporate bulk cards follow the business contract.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the gift-cards operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.

## Agent checklist

- Confirm the request is about lost and stolen gift cards.
- Check the order, account, or transaction state.
- Apply the threshold or timing rule documented in this archived page.
- Escalate cases outside the documented path.
