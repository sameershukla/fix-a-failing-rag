# Archived 2025 Copy: Redeeming Gift Cards

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered redeeming gift cards before the 2026 policy consolidation. This article explains how gift card balances are applied at checkout and what happens when the card value is higher or lower than the order total.

## Archived rule

Customers can apply up to 8 BrightCart gift cards to one order. Any unused balance remains on the card for future use. If the gift card total is less than the order total, the customer can pay the remainder with another supported payment method.

## Eligibility details

- The gift card must be active and have a positive balance.
- The checkout displays the amount taken from each card.
- Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash through normal checkout.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer has gift cards with $30 and $40 balances and buys a $50 item. Checkout can use $25 from the first card and $25 from the second, leaving $15 on the second card.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Fraud locked gift cards cannot be redeemed until reviewed.
- Some restricted products may not accept promotional credits, but normal gift cards remain valid.
- More than three gift cards requires customer care assistance and is not guaranteed.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the gift-cards operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.
