# Archived 2025 Copy: Gift Card Purchase Rules

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered gift card purchase rules before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy covers digital BrightCart gift card purchases. Gift cards are delivered by email and can be used for eligible BrightCart purchases.

## Archived rule

A single BrightCart gift card can be purchased in an amount from $10 through $500. Digital delivery usually occurs within 30 minutes after payment approval. Gift cards cannot be purchased using another gift card.

## Eligibility details

- The buyer must provide a valid recipient email address.
- Fraud screening can delay delivery.
- The purchase is recorded as a separate gift card order.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A customer buys a $100 gift card at 10:00 AM with an approved credit card. The recipient normally receives the delivery email by 10:15 AM unless the order is held for review.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- Bulk corporate gift card orders use the business sales process.
- A $10 or $700 consumer gift card is outside the allowed range.
- Promotional credits are not gift cards.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the gift-cards operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.
