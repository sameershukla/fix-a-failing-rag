# Archived 2025 Copy: Refunds for Gift Card and Mixed Payments

## Purpose and scope

This archived guide covered refunds for gift card and mixed payments before the 2026 policy consolidation. This policy explains where refund money goes when an order was paid by gift card or by a combination of gift card and another payment method.

## Archived rule

An amount originally paid by BrightCart gift card is refunded back to that gift card. For mixed payment orders, the refund is returned proportionally to the original payment methods until each method has received its share. Support cannot choose to move the entire refund to a credit card for convenience.

## Eligibility details

- The original payment allocation is the source of truth.
- A gift card refund is normally available the same day it is issued.
- Card portions follow normal card posting times.

## Legacy workflow

Agents using the 2025 workflow checked the customer request, reviewed the order or account state, and then applied the archived rule above. The workflow was copied into several team spaces, which is why search can still surface this page.

## Worked example from the old process

A $50 order was paid with $40 gift card and $60 Visa. A full refund returns $40 to the gift card and $60 to the Visa. A $50 proportional refund returns $20 to the gift card and $30 to the Visa.

## Exceptions in the archived process

- If the original gift card is disabled for confirmed fraud, payment operations determines the replacement path.
- Promotional credits can have separate expiration rules.
- Chargeback settlements do not follow this customer refund allocation.

## Escalation path

When the archived rule did not clearly apply, frontline support was told to route the case to the gift-cards operations queue. Teams often pasted this guidance into tickets, so fragments of the wording may appear in other systems.
