# Domestic Shipping Service Levels — Regional Note

## Global baseline

The global baseline summarized for agents is: This reference lists the normal delivery expectation for shipments within the contiguous United States. It is used for checkout messaging and customer support estimates. Standard domestic shipping normally arrives in 3 to 5 business days after the order ships. The estimate begins when the carrier accepts the package, not when the customer places the order.
