# Expedited Shipping — Regional Note

## Global baseline

The global baseline summarized for agents is: This article describes the BrightCart two business day shipping option. It explains the order cutoff and what the service commitment does and does not include. Eligible expedited orders placed before 2:00 PM Central Time on a business day are expected to ship the same day and arrive within 2 business days after carrier acceptance. Orders placed after the cutoff are processed the next business day. Regional teams must start from that rule and then apply local obligations or carrier/bank constraints.
