## Global baseline

The global baseline summarized for agents is: This runbook covers claims that a gift card code was lost, deleted, or stolen. Gift cards are cash like instruments, so replacement requires evidence of ownership. BrightCart may replace a lost digital gift card only when the claimant can verify the original purchase or recipient information and the remaining balance has not been spent. Gift cards are not redeemable for cash and spent balances are not restored… Regional teams must start from that rule and then apply local obligations or carrier/bank constraints.
