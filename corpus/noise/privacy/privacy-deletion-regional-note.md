# Privacy Deletion Requests — Regional Note

## Global baseline

The global baseline summarized for agents is: This policy covers privacy requests asking BrightCart to delete eligible personal data. It complements, but is broader than, normal account closure. BrightCart verifies the requester before processing a privacy deletion request and targets completion within 30 days.
