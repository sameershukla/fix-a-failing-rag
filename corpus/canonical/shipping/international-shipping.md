# International Shipping and Duties

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains normal international delivery timing and customer responsibility for import charges. It applies only to countries shown as eligible at checkout.

## Standard rule

International orders normally arrive in 7 to 14 business days after shipment. Import duties, taxes, and customs fees are the customer responsibility unless checkout explicitly states that duties are prepaid. Customs processing can add time outside BrightCart control.

## Conditions and decision points

- The destination country must be supported at checkout.
- Carrier tracking may pause while a package is in customs.
- The customer is responsible for accurate recipient and tax information.

## Worked example

A package ships from the United States to France and reaches customs after six business days. The carrier requests a local import tax before final delivery. The customer pays that fee because the order was not marked duties prepaid.

## Exceptions and edge cases

- Some promotions include duties prepaid and state that explicitly on the order.
- Restricted products cannot be shipped to certain countries.
- Military and diplomatic addresses use a separate carrier route and may take longer.

## Operational notes

Support should not promise a customs release date. If tracking shows a customs document request, direct the customer to provide the requested information to the carrier.
