# Electronics Return Rules

## Purpose and scope

Electronics have a shorter return window because activated or opened devices can lose resale value. This article applies to headphones, tablets, smart home devices, cameras, and similar products listed as electronics.

## Standard rule

Electronics may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. Opened electronics are not returnable for preference changes, but defective electronics can still be returned or replaced during the 14 day period. Unopened electronics can be returned for any standard eligible reason.

## Conditions and decision points

- The serial number must match the order record.
- Factory accessories should be included.
- A device reported as defective may be tested after arrival.

## Worked example

A customer opens wireless headphones, uses them for two days, and decides the color is not ideal. That is a preference return and is not eligible because the electronics were opened. If one earbud does not power on, the defective item path applies.

## Exceptions and edge cases

- Carrier damaged electronics should use the damaged item process.
- Manufacturer warranty support may apply after the BrightCart return window.
- Items marked final sale remain subject to the final sale rule except for damage or incorrect shipment.

## Operational notes

Support should note whether the box was opened and whether the reason is preference, defect, damage, or wrong item. Do not treat all opened electronics as automatically defective.
