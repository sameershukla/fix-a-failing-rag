# Privacy Deletion Requests

## Purpose and scope

This policy covers privacy requests asking BrightCart to delete eligible personal data. It complements, but is broader than, normal account closure.

## Standard rule

BrightCart verifies the requester before processing a privacy deletion request and targets completion within 30 days. Data required for tax, fraud prevention, legal claims, or other mandatory retention can be kept in restricted systems for the required period.

## Conditions and decision points

- The request must be linked to a verified identity.
- The privacy team tracks completion across source systems.
- Open disputes can create a retention requirement.

## Worked example

A customer requests privacy deletion after closing the account. BrightCart removes eligible profile and preference data, while retaining required invoice and fraud records in restricted systems until their retention periods expire.

## Exceptions and edge cases

- A legal hold overrides routine deletion for covered records.
- Backups can age out according to the backup retention schedule rather than immediate live deletion.
- Data controlled solely by another company follows that company's process.

## Operational notes

Support should route formal privacy deletion requests to the privacy workflow and avoid promising that every record will disappear immediately.
